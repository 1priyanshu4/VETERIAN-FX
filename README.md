# veterian-fx

**Protect Your Funded Account. Trade Smarter. Get Paid Faster.**

veterian-fx is a complete trading companion built especially for prop firm traders. It is designed to make the entire funded account journey simple, safe, and stress-free.

> **Short version:**  
> veterian-fx protects your prop firm funded accounts, stops rule breaches, gives AI trade guidance, copy trading, money management & psychology support, free funded account alerts, prop firm friendly bots, and also provides ready broker accounts with fast crypto deposits & withdrawals.

---

## 9 Core Features & Directory Architecture

1. **Prop Firm Guard (Rule Protection)**
   - Page: `accounts.html` / `accounts/index.html` & `dashboard.html`
   - Real-time tracking of Daily Loss, Max Drawdown, Profit Target, Consistency, and other rules.
   - Clear warnings before any rule is about to be broken with color-coded status (Safe, Warning, Danger).

2. **Smart Trading Guidance**
   - Page: `crypto.html` / `crypto/index.html`
   - Exact lot size calculation based on account balance and risk.
   - Money Management recommendations and Trading Psychology checks to prevent revenge trading and overtrading.

3. **AI Analyser**
   - Page: `analytics.html` / `analytics/index.html`
   - Continuous market and account analysis with high-accuracy trade suggestions (Entry, Stop Loss, Take Profit) and simple reasoning.

4. **Copy Trading**
   - Page: `investments.html` / `investments/index.html`
   - Follow and copy top prop-firm friendly traders with automatic risk rule enforcement and instant toggle controls.

5. **News & Risk Alerts**
   - Page: `notifications.html` / `notifications/index.html`
   - High-impact news alerts, red-folder warnings, and spread spike notifications before rule-breaching events.

6. **Payout Journey Support**
   - Page: `budgets.html` / `budgets/index.html`
   - Step-by-step guidance toward profit targets, consistency rules, and seamless payout clearance.

7. **Free Funded Account Alerts**
   - Live giveaway alerts, promo codes, and free prop firm challenge passes.

8. **Prop Firm Friendly Bots**
   - Page: `cards.html` / `cards/index.html`
   - Curated marketplace of rule-respecting EAs and automated bots available on weekly, monthly, or lifetime licenses.

9. **Broker Account Service**
   - Page: `transfers.html` / `transfers/index.html`
   - Ready-to-use trading accounts available for purchase when brokers pause new registrations. Multi-account options with fast, hassle-free Crypto-only (USDT/BTC/ETH) deposits & withdrawals.
